Gridsome, the static site generator for Vue.js based on Webpack and Babel, was born roughly eight years ago and (*let's face it*) died four years later on the dawn of Vue 3 and Vite era. Many people (*myself included*) struggled to accept that but time had shown that there's no other choice than either to stay and suffer the obsoleting ecosystem rot, or to migrate to [some alternative project](https://fyodor.io/migration-from-gridsome-to-astro/).

I had personally chosen the former eventually and up until recently lived quite well with that, taking into account the modest requirements of [my personal Gridsome-based website](https://fyodor.io). I used Bun to [keep the project alive](https://fyodor.io/how-bun-can-help-to-revive-a-gridsome-project/) and tried to find some bearable HTML+CSS+JS alternative in parallel.

Long story short, I failed (*Astro is too hyperactive, Jekyll is too Ruby, Hugo is too Go, and so on*) and dealing with Bun was [quite depressing](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/issues/16766) lately, so one weekend day I decided to check how hard would that be to try to update Gridsome with LTS Node and modernized dependencies in mind, preserving the same simplicity philosophy at the same time.

So that's how I came to this fork, and so far it works well for me, taking into account the updated alpha version supports two websites — [this docs project](https://gridmix.github.io), and my actual [personal website](https://fyodor.io).

My goals with Gridsome is to at least avoid migrations for the latter in foreseeable future and to maintain healthy ecosystem to avoid the ubiquitous security problems `npm` brought the recent years (*or even days*).

If you have any feedback/suggestions, please provide it through [the core repository issues](https://github.com/gridmix/gridmix/issues), my [personal socials](https://fyodor.io/links), or via [ping@fyodor.io](mailto:ping@fyodor.io).
